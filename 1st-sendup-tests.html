The complete Class 9 1st Send-Up exam series by KEPS Science Academy, Ahmad Pur Sial — all subjects in BISE Faisalabad board pattern, free PDF download for every student preparing for the 9th class send-up exams and annual board exams.

Download the 9th class 1st Send-Up test papers for all eight subjects below. Every send-up paper follows the BISE pattern: the objective part (MCQs) and the subjective part are printed on one sheet — front and back (duplex print) — except Tarjuma-tul-Quran, which is a complete one-page test. These Class 9 send-up exam papers cover the taught syllabus, including the smart syllabus chapters for Chemistry and Biology, and are ideal practice before the annual board examination.
