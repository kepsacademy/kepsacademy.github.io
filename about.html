About KEPS Science Academy

An evening academy in Ahmad Pur Sial, District Jhang, dedicated to board-exam success for the students of our area.

Our Story

KEPS Science Academy was founded in Ahmad Pur Sial with a simple belief: students of our area deserve the same quality of exam preparation found in big cities. Every class here is built around the board examination — what the paper demands, how marks are awarded, and how a student can secure the maximum.

Teaching is paired with a disciplined testing system: weekly tests, full BISE-pattern papers, and personal follow-up, so no student is ever left guessing about their preparation level.

Meet the Principal

MA

Muhammad Aqeel

Principal — MPhil Mathematics

Sir Muhammad Aqeel is an experienced teacher who leads every batch at KEPS personally. He prepares the academy's own BISE-pattern test papers and study notes, so students practise on material designed exactly like the board exams they will face. His teaching focuses on clear concepts, exam technique, and the confidence that comes from thorough preparation.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To give every student of Ahmad Pur Sial honest, exam-focused teaching — clear concepts, regular testing, and personal guidance — so board results reflect their true effort.

Our Vision

To be the academy where the toppers of Ahmad Pur Sial are made — known across District Jhang for discipline, results, and the trust of parents.
